## 📌 Problem Statement
Farmers often recognize crop diseases or pest infestations only after visible damage has spread. Delayed diagnosis leads to excessive pesticide use, higher costs, and yield loss. The challenge is to provide timely, reliable, and locally relevant detection, forecasting, and management support.

## 🚀 Proposed Solution
A farmer-friendly crop-health system that supports image-based symptom identification, weather-based risk forecasting, and multilingual advisories. The platform combines AI models for disease detection with geospatial hotspot mapping to provide actionable, farm-level alerts and integrate pest management actions.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
*   **AI/ML:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn), TensorFlow & Keras, Google Colab
*   **Frontend:** JavaScript (ES6), Tailwind CSS, Leaflet.js Mapping Engine, HTML5
*   **Backend & Cloud:** Supabase PostgreSQL, JSON/Async Fetch Pipeline, Offline-first Local Storage
*   **APIs & Integrations:** WeatherAPI, IoT Sensors (Planned for precise micro-climate data)
*   **Hosting:** Vercel

## ✨ Key Features
*   **Image-Based Detection:** AI-driven symptom identification from field photos.
*   **Risk Forecasting:** WeatherAPI integration to predict local infestation risks.
*   **Geospatial Mapping:** Leaflet.js dashboard mapping disease hotspots for officials.
*   **Offline-First Architecture:** Local storage buffering for rural areas with low connectivity.
*   **Multilingual Advisories:** Localized guidance on safe pesticide use and interventions.
